## Featured work

### 🌷 [Bloom v3](https://github.com/kandukurinomusubramanyachary-ai/BLOOMv3)

A cross-platform health and wellbeing product for people navigating PCOS, PCOD and irregular cycles.

`React Native` · `Expo` · `Firebase` · `Express` · `AI orchestration` · `MediaPipe`

- Daily cycle, symptom, diet and wellbeing tracking
- **Meg V2** with routing, safety checks, memory, fallback providers and telemetry
- Camera-based strength experiences with pose estimation
- Account-scoped storage and privacy-first architecture
- Android, iOS and web from one codebase

### 🛰️ [EarthPulse](https://github.com/kandukurinomusubramanyachary-ai/EARTHPULSE)

An AI-powered geospatial intelligence prototype that turns natural-language questions into evidence-backed satellite change analysis.

`Python` · `FastAPI` · `Sentinel-2` · `STAC` · `Rasterio` · `Geospatial AI`

- Streams real Sentinel-2 satellite imagery instead of mock data
- Detects urban expansion, vegetation loss and water-body change
- Rejects false change using spectral, seasonal and persistence filters
- Produces measurements, confidence scores and explainable evidence
